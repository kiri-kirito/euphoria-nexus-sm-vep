# Euphoria Nexus - Master Defect & Feature Tracker

## Priority 0: Critical Core Failures (Must Fix Immediately)

### Authentication & Access
* Users cannot log in after registration and receive an "Email not confirmed" error[cite: 1, 4, 5].
* Creating more than one account triggers an "Email Rate Limit" exceeded error[cite: 5].
* The forgot password and message options are malfunctioning[cite: 2].

### Cart, Checkout & Orders
* The system returns a "Your cart is empty" message immediately after an order is placed[cite: 4].
* Recent orders completely fail to appear in the tracking and orders tab[cite: 5].
* The location selection gets permanently stuck in the Dhaka region during checkout[cite: 4].
* Modifying the region within the supplier zone has no effect on the product catalog[cite: 5].

## Priority 1: High-Impact Functional Bugs

### Vendor & Seller Operations
* Sellers are completely unable to use the edit button on products within the "My Products" section[cite: 5].
* There is no functionality allowing sellers to collaborate and create bundles with other sellers[cite: 5].
* The "Negotiate price" feature is broken on the product pages[cite: 5].
* Seller payout timeframes are displaying incorrectly and do not match Bangladesh UTC[cite: 5].

### Support & Delivery Management
* Support agents encounter a "ticket not found" error when attempting to reply to queues[cite: 5].
* The platform lacks any option to resolve complaints within the overview section[cite: 5].
* The entire chat and support system is currently non-functional and cannot be tested[cite: 1, 5].
* The message complete and reply functionalities do not work[cite: 2].
* Clicking "Manage" or "view details & navigate" on assigned deliveries produces no action[cite: 5].
* The "Become a Delivery Agent" registration option is broken[cite: 5].
* Delivery worker assignment is flawed[cite: 5]. 
* Workers should be restricted to accepting deliveries only within their assigned regional zones like Dhaka or Dhanmondi[cite: 5].

## Priority 2: UI/UX & Layout Breakages

### Interface & Navigation
* The main header bar containing the search, cart, icon, and logout elements duplicates itself when viewing the orders or wishlist pages[cite: 4, 5].
* The wishlist functionality is fundamentally broken on the buyer side[cite: 1].
* Featured value bundles completely lack the option to be added to a wishlist[cite: 5].
* Product images are missing from featured bundles, such as the iPhone in the Apple/Samsung bundle[cite: 1, 5].
* The "Industrial" product category is missing all photographic assets[cite: 1].
